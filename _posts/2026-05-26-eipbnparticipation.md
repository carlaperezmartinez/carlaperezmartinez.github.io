Shaun and Carla attended the 69th International Conference on Electron, Ion, Photon, Beams and Nanofabrication in Denver, CO. Shaun presented a poster on "Ion Implantation into Semiconductors Using Ionic Liquid Ion Sources." Carla is a member of the steering committee for the conference and she led the short courses, chaired technical sessions, and was the moderator for a panel discussion on "Maximising AI and human efforts towards precision nanomanufacturing and metrology."
