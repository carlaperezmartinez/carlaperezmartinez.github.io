# Join FABILIS

We are always looking for talented and motivated individuals to join the FABILIS lab. If you are interested in contributing to groundbreaking research in ionic liquid ion sources, we would love to hear from you. To apply, please send your CV and a brief statement of interest to Dr. Carla Perez-Martinez at carlaperezmartinez@ucl.ac.uk.

## Postdoctoral Fellowship Opportunities

Dr. Perez-Martinez is happy to advise and support interested candidates who wish to apply for postdoctoral fellowships (e.g. Royal Commission of 1851, Newton International Fellowship). Please contact us well in advance the submission deadline to discuss possible research projects and process logistics. Please note that we are an applied physics laboratory, and we particulary value candidates with expertise on vacuum technologies, ion beam systems, and other physics instrumentation.
