# Join FABILIS

We are always looking for talented and motivated individuals to join the FABILIS lab. If you are interested in contributing to groundbreaking research in ionic liquid ion sources, we would love to hear from you. To apply, please send your CV and a brief statement of interest to Dr. Carla Perez-Martinez at carlaperezmartinez@ucl.ac.uk. 


## PhD Opportunities 

### [UCL EPSRC Landscape Award (UELA)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/epsrc-doctoral-training/prospective-students/ucl-epsrc-landscape-award-uela-studentships) — *New!*
**Fully funded for 4 years** (fees + enhanced stipend + additional research training and travel support).  
Open to **Home Applicants Only**.  
 
➡️ **Our project:** *Building the future of chipmaking with smarter etching*  [Link to Project Description](https://ucl-epsrc-dtp.github.io/2026-27-project-catalogue/projects/2531bd1675.html)  

📌Students apply centrally to the UCL EPSRC UELA Award, and indicate interest for our project (2531bd1675) in part C of the application.

📅 **Application deadline:** *13:00 UK time, 21-May-2026.*

This project involves both computational 🖥️ and experimental work 🔬, is multidisciplinary, and will start in September 2026. I care deeply about my students’ wellbeing and professional development and hope that you may consider applying for this project.

## Postdoctoral Fellowship Opportunities

Dr. Perez-Martinez is happy to advise and support interested candidates who wish to apply for postdoctoral fellowships (e.g. Royal Commission of 1851, Newton International Fellowship). Please contact us well in advance the submission deadline to discuss possible research projects and process logistics.
